How to play

Drag the joystick, bottom right, to fly. Tap and hold FIRE, bottom left, to shoot. On a keyboard: WASD or arrows to fly, space to fire.

Every missile hit splits a rock in two. The halves are smaller and quicker. Three lives. Space is endless — the belt is not.
